About

What WEAVE does

WEAVE building design is a residential building design practice based in Denmark, Western Australia, specialising in healthy, climate-responsive and functional homes.

WEAVE provides the design foundations that matter most: a well-considered floor plan, an optimised building envelope, and documentation for approvals and construction. It's a focused service that gives you clarity and confidence to move forward, without the full scope of architectural services such as contract administration and on-site project management. This means lower professional fees, but it also means you'll take on more of the coordination role during construction.

Services include

  • Custom home design and drafting (Denmark WA and surrounds)
  • Advice on healthy construction materials and practices
  • Pre-designed plans that consider climate and orientation

What guides the work

Every project is approached with a focus on simplicity, functionality, healthy building practices, energy efficiency, and a genuine connection to your site. The goal is always a home that works for your life, not just on paper.

The process

From design to construction

Following are the stages involved in getting a project from design to construction. You may engage WEAVE for all stages or just the ones relevant to your needs.

1

Concept Stage

Following an initial consultation, a comprehensive brief aligned with your values and a fee proposal will be prepared. A site plan and analysis will be prepared using existing information and on-site measurements. If required, a bushfire attack level (BAL) assessment and feature or contour survey may also be needed. Drawings are then prepared to convey the preliminary concept design. The design aims to combine your values and needs with WEAVE's principles of simplicity, small but spacious rooms, functional relationships, healthy building practices, energy efficiency, solar passive design, and connection to your site.

Typical inclusions

  • Value-aligned brief
  • Site analysis and documentation of existing conditions
  • Concept design drawings: site plan, floor plan, section
  • In-person or online meeting
  • Revised concept drawings: site plan, floor plan, elevations, section
2

Planning Approval Stage

Following the concept drawings, the design is further developed. You are encouraged to be part of this process and may involve other professionals at this stage. It is recommended to test the thermal performance of the design and obtain cost advice at this point. Once finalised, a set of drawings is prepared for council planning approval if required.

Typical inclusions

  • Liaison with consultants and relevant authorities
  • Design development drawings: site plan, floor plan, section, internal and external 3D views
  • Preliminary NatHERS energy assessment to optimise design (recommended)
  • Planning approval drawings: site plan, floor plan, elevations, section
  • In-person or online meeting
3

Building Approval Stage

This stage develops the approved design into a detailed set of drawings suitable for building certification, builder quoting, and construction. While the primary purpose is to obtain a building permit, WEAVE's documentation goes further, supporting a coordinated and efficient build by resolving key design decisions early and clearly identifying required selections, reducing uncertainty during construction.

Typical inclusions

  • Liaison with consultants and relevant authorities
  • Design development drawings: site plan, floor plan, elevations, 3D views, detail section, wet area internal elevations, window and door schedule, electrical plan
  • Building approval drawings: site plan, stormwater plan, floor plan, elevations, detail section, specifications
  • NatHERS energy assessment
  • In-person or online meeting
4

Construction

WEAVE's standard service concludes at the completion of the Building Approval Stage. Construction administration and on-site project management are not included. The building permit drawings are intended for certification, builder pricing, and a smooth construction process, including insulation, glazing, waterproofing, internal elevations, window and door schedules, and confirmed or base selections for fittings and finishes.

If design advice, drawing amendments, or clarification is required during construction, these services can be provided as a separate, clearly scoped service by agreement. Both PDF and CAD files will be supplied to ensure a smooth handover to any other professionals you choose to engage.

Regional availability

  • Building permit and planning approval services are available for projects in and around Denmark, Western Australia
  • For projects outside this region, WEAVE offers concept design, project review and advice services only
  • Concept drawings and advice can be taken to a local building professional for further development

Design considerations

Things to think about

The following themes are worth reflecting on as you develop your brief. They can help clarify what matters most to you and shape the kind of home you want to create.

Shelter

  • Protection from the elements
  • Comfort
  • Support for daily life

Sustainability

  • Passive design principles
  • Build less and smaller
  • Environmentally friendly materials
  • Flexibility and accessibility
  • Consideration of maintenance

Health

  • Ventilation and moisture control
  • Sunlight
  • Low-toxicity materials and finishes
  • Active design to facilitate movement

Beauty

  • Balance and simplicity
  • Connection to the outdoors
  • Responding to place and circumstance

Cost

  • Assess desires, needs and wants
  • Balance cost and lifestyle
  • Build less and smaller

Water wise landscaping

  • Permeable surfaces
  • Rainwater tanks
  • Swales
  • Mulch
  • Selective plant species
